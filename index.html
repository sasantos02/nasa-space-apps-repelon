Explore technical indicators and sources

Vulnerability (0–1): Sentinel-2 vegetation signals (NDVI: greenness; NDMI: vegetation moisture; SAVI: soil-adjusted greenness). Hazard (0–1): CHIRPS V3 relative rainfall deficit. Risk index = 50% hazard + 50% vulnerability. Outside the irrigation district does not necessarily mean no irrigation.

Limits: current crops, effective irrigation, yields and stored soil water are unknown. Regional crop scenarios are not parcel-level recommendations. Sensitivity frequencies are not harvest probabilities.

Edit the crop, start month, and post-harvest land cover in each option. These are illustrative sequences, not validated agronomic rotations.

Why these calendars are exploratory

Only April–June sowing dates were modeled. The 120-day maize/sorghum and 210-day cassava cycles are reference cases; actual crop durations vary. A cover or fallow period is a management placeholder with no modeled water, soil or yield benefit. We have not evaluated a real farm's current rotation.
