Farmer priorities → scenario reading

Scores are simple shares computed from the calendars (e.g., water-needing months that are not dry). Plans without a crop score 0 on “sowing with rains”. This is a transparent reading of assumptions, not a yield or income model; soil health is reduced to months with cover.

What do these terms mean?

Vulnerability (0–1): how dry or sparse the vegetation looks from Sentinel-2 (NDVI = greenness, NDMI = moisture, SAVI = greenness corrected for soil). Hazard (0–1): how far recent rain fell below the 10-year normal (NASA GPM IMERG). Risk = half hazard + half vulnerability. Irrigation district: area served by the ASUREP district registry; “outside” does not mean rainfed.

Limits: no parcel-level crop data; High class mostly tiny bare-soil lots (not crops); ranking is robust to ±20 % changes, class counts are not; farmer-priority weights are adjustable assumptions.
